01 / Overview

A score is more than timing.

Instead of requiring phoneme-level durations or a time-aligned F0 curve, VocalRender accepts the symbolic language composers already use. The model is free to realize natural phrasing while remaining faithful to the written score.

01

Interleaved score

Lyrics and pitch–duration pairs preserve note-to-word alignment, including melisma.

02

Continuous acoustics

Compact AudioVAE latents retain pitch, timbre, articulation, and fine acoustic detail.

03

Expressive rendering

Autoregressive diffusion models global phrasing and reconstructs local detail.
