## Quality controls

- Primary sources were preferred over reviews and model roundups.
- Author-reported audio rankings were not promoted to independent facts.
- Release status, URLs, pricing, and GPU visibility were checked live.
- Strong paper-only models were placed on the watchlist, not treated as available.
- Conflicts were preserved where evaluation protocols were incomparable.
- The recommendation is based on availability, fit, optionality, and risk—not a claim that the models were acoustically tested during this research run.

## Known limitations

- No new model was installed or listened to in this research phase.
- Commercial products expose limited architecture and training-data detail.
- Current pricing and preview availability can change.
- The local baseline is one voice and a small evaluation set.
- Exact MiniMax Music3 memory fit remains untested because only one GPU was visible.
